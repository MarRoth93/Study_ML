## Vector Operations

* **Addition (commutative):**
  \$r + s = s + r\$

* **Scalar Multiplication:**
  \$2r = r + r\$

* **Vector Norm (Euclidean norm):**
  \$|r|^2 = \sum\_i r\_i^2\$

* **Dot Product (Inner Product):**
  \$r \cdot s = \sum\_i r\_i s\_i\$

Properties of dot product:

* Commutative:
  \$r \cdot s = s \cdot r\$

* Distributive:
  \$r \cdot (s + t) = r \cdot s + r \cdot t\$

* Associative with scalar multiplication:
  \$r \cdot (a s) = a (r \cdot s)\$

* Relationship with norm:
  \$r \cdot r = |r|^2\$

* Geometric Interpretation:
  \$r \cdot s = |r||s|\cos\theta\$

* **Scalar and Vector Projection:**

Scalar projection of \$s\$ onto \$r\$:
\$\frac{r \cdot s}{|r|}\$

Vector projection of \$s\$ onto \$r\$:
\$\frac{r \cdot s}{r \cdot r} r\$

---

## Basis

A basis is a set of \$n\$ vectors that:

1. are not linear combinations of each other (linear independence),
2. span the space.

The space is then \$n\$-dimensional.

## Matrices

* **Matrix Multiplication (2x2 matrix and vector):**
  \$
  \begin{pmatrix}
  a & b \\
  c & d
  \end{pmatrix}
  \begin{pmatrix}
  e \\
  f
  \end{pmatrix}
  \=
  \begin{pmatrix}
  ae + bf \\
  ce + df
  \end{pmatrix}
  \$

* **Scalar multiplication of matrices:**
  \$A(nr) = n(Ar) = nr'\$

* **Distributive property:**
  \$A(r + s) = Ar + As\$

* **Identity matrix (2x2):**
  \$
  I =
  \begin{pmatrix}
  1 & 0 \\
  0 & 1
  \end{pmatrix}
  \$

* **Clockwise rotation by angle \$\theta\$:**
  \$
  \begin{pmatrix}
  \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\
  -\sin\theta & \cos\theta
  \end{pmatrix}
  \$

* **Determinant of 2x2 matrix:**
  \$
  \det\begin{pmatrix}
  a & b \\
  c & d
  \end{pmatrix} = ad - bc
  \$

* **Inverse of 2x2 matrix:**
  \$
  \begin{pmatrix}
  a & b \\
  c & d
  \end{pmatrix}^{-1} =
  \frac{1}{ad - bc}
  \begin{pmatrix}
  d & -b \\
  -c & a
  \end{pmatrix}
  \$

* **Matrix multiplication (summation convention):**
  \$(ab)*{ik} = \sum\_j a*{ij}b\_{jk}\$

## Change of Basis

To change from an original basis to a new, primed basis, let the columns of the transformation matrix \$B\$ be the new basis vectors in the original coordinate system:

* Forward transformation:
  \$B r' = r\$

* Reverse transformation:
  \$r' = B^{-1} r\$

If a matrix \$A\$ is orthonormal (all columns are unit vectors and orthogonal), then:
\$A^T = A^{-1}\$

---

## Gram–Schmidt Process for Orthonormal Basis

Given \$n\$ linearly independent vectors \${v\_1, v\_2, \dots, v\_n}\$, the orthonormal basis is obtained as:

* First basis vector:
  \$e\_1 = \frac{v\_1}{|v\_1|}\$

* Second basis vector:
  \$u\_2 = v\_2 - (v\_2 \cdot e\_1)e\_1,\quad e\_2 = \frac{u\_2}{|u\_2|}\$

* Similarly, continue for subsequent vectors:
  \$u\_k = v\_k - \sum\_{j=1}^{k-1}(v\_k \cdot e\_j)e\_j,\quad e\_k = \frac{u\_k}{|u\_k|}\$

---

## Transformations (Reflections, Rotations, etc.)

For a transformation (e.g., reflection) in a new basis \$E\$:

1. Transform into the basis \$E^{-1}\$.
2. Perform the transformation \$T\_E\$ in the new basis.
3. Transform back to original basis \$E\$.

Resulting transformation:
\$r' = E T\_E E^{-1} r\$

## Eigenstuff (Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues)

To investigate matrix \$A\$ (size \$n\times n\$), solve:
\$A x = \lambda x\$

where \$\lambda\$ is the scalar eigenvalue. Eigenvalues satisfy:
\$(A - \lambda I)x = 0\$

where \$I\$ is the \$n\times n\$ identity matrix.

---

### PageRank Algorithm

The dominant eigenvector of the link matrix \$L\$ can be found using the Power Method:

* Iterative method without damping:
  \$r\_{i+1} = L r\_i\$

* Iterative method with damping factor \$d\$ (stabilization):
  \$r\_{i+1} = d L r\_i + \frac{1 - d}{n}\$
