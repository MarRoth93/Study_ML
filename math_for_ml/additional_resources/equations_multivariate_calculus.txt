### Definition of a derivative

$$ f'(x) = \frac{df(x)}{dx} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \left( \frac{f(x+\Delta x) - f(x)}{\Delta x} \right) $$

---

### Time saving rules

- **Sum Rule:**
  $$ \frac{d}{dx} \big(f(x) + g(x)\big) = \frac{d}{dx} f(x) + \frac{d}{dx} g(x) $$

- **Power Rule:**
  $$ f(x) = ax^b \quad \Rightarrow \quad f'(x) = abx^{b-1} $$

- **Product Rule:**
  $$ A(x) = f(x)g(x) \quad \Rightarrow \quad A'(x) = f'(x)g(x) + f(x)g'(x) $$

- **Chain Rule:**
  If $h = h(p)$ and $p = p(m)$, then
  $$ \frac{dh}{dm} = \frac{dh}{dp} \cdot \frac{dp}{dm} $$

- **Total derivative:**
  For $f(x, y, z, \dots)$, where each variable depends on parameter $t$,
  $$ \frac{df}{dt} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \frac{dx}{dt} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \frac{dy}{dt} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial z} \frac{dz}{dt} + \dots $$

---

### Derivatives of named functions

1. $$ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \frac{1}{x} = -\frac{1}{x^2} $$
2. $$ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \sin x = \cos x $$
3. $$ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \cos x = -\sin x $$
4. $$ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} e^x = e^x $$

---

### Derivative structures

Let $f = f(x,y,z)$.

- **Jacobian:**
  $$ J_f = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial z} \end{bmatrix} $$

- **Hessian:**
  $$ H_f = \begin{bmatrix}
  \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2} & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x \partial y} & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x \partial z} \\
  \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y \partial x} & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2} & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y \partial z} \\
  \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial z \partial x} & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial z \partial y} & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial z^2}
  \end{bmatrix} $$

---

### Taylor Series

- **Univariate:**
  $$ f(x) = f(c) + f'(c)(x-c) + \frac{1}{2} f''(c)(x-c)^2 + \dots = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(c)}{n!} (x-c)^n $$

- **Multivariate:**
  $$ f(x) = f(c) + J_f(c)(x-c) + \frac{1}{2} (x-c)^T H_f(c) (x-c) + \dots $$

### Optimization and Vector Calculus

- **Newton-Raphson:**
  $$ x_{i+1} = x_i - \frac{f(x_i)}{f'(x_i)} $$

- **Gradient:**
  $$ \nabla f = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \\ \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \\ \frac{\partial f}{\partial z} \end{bmatrix} $$

- **Directional Gradient:**
  $$ \nabla f \cdot \hat{r} $$

- **Gradient Descent:**
  $$ s_{n+1} = s_n - \gamma \nabla f $$

- **Lagrange Multipliers ($\lambda$):**
  $$ \nabla f = \lambda \nabla g $$

- **Least Squares — $\chi^2$ minimization:**
  $$ \chi^2 = \sum_i \frac{(y_i - y(x_i; a_k))^2}{\sigma_i} $$
  Criterion: $\nabla \chi^2 = 0$

  Update rule:
  $$ a_{\text{next}} = a_{\text{cur}} - \gamma \nabla \chi^2 $$
  $$ = a_{\text{cur}} + \gamma \sum_i \frac{(y_i - y(x_i; a_k))}{\sigma_i} \frac{\partial y}{\partial a_k} $$
